Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 17, 2026

Parsha Map ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the Parsha Map website and iOS app.

Information We Collect

We use Umami Analytics, a privacy-focused analytics tool, to understand how users interact with Parsha Map. Umami does not use cookies and does not collect personally identifiable information. The data collected includes:

  • Pages visited and features used
  • General device type (mobile or desktop)
  • Country-level location (not precise location)
  • Referring website

This data is anonymous, aggregated, and never linked to any individual user.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require account registration or collect names, emails, or passwords.
  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or sell data to third parties.
  • We do not use cookies.

Third-Party Services

Parsha Map connects to the following external services to provide content:

  • Sefaria — Jewish text library (sefaria.org)
  • Wikipedia — Historical context
  • The Metropolitan Museum of Art — Archaeological artifact images
  • OpenStreetMap / CartoDB — Map tiles

These services have their own privacy policies. Parsha Map does not share any user data with them beyond standard HTTP requests.

Children's Privacy

Parsha Map does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
