## Event → source map (quotes are in the next two sections)

- c. 1275 Louvre Leather Roll → Bietak & Rendsburg p. 29: "The most detailed and elaborate record of brickmaking is the Louvre Leather Roll, dated to year 5 of Ramesses II, with various entries listed by a taskmaster. A sample entry reads: 'Yupa son of Urhiya, (target) 2,000 bricks: 660 arrived, 410 arrived, 560 arrived / total 1,630 / deficit 370.'" Year 5 of Ramesses II (acc. 1279, low chronology) = c. 1275. NOTE: their summary list (p. 40) instead says the roll is "dated to the 19th Dynasty generally".
