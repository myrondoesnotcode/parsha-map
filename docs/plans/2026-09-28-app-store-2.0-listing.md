## Description

Parsha Map is a companion to the weekly Torah portion. Open it any day of the week to see this Shabbat's parsha, where it happens on the map, and what to talk about at the table.

• This week: the parsha for the coming Shabbat, its date, and candle-lighting times for New York or Jerusalem (Diaspora or Israel reading).
• Stories: short, map-guided stories that walk through the parsha card by card, starting with the book of Genesis.
• The Shabbat table: three questions after each story, one for kids, one for everyone and one to go deeper.
• Read: the full text in Hebrew and English from Sefaria, a short summary, notes from Jewish tradition, and historical background.
• The map: the places named in each week's reading, on a map with 3D terrain. Sites that are uncertain or unknown are labelled that way.
• Dates, two ways: each parsha shows the range scholars often propose and the date in Jewish tradition, and says when the Torah's events can't be dated.
• Sources: tap a verse reference in a story to see the verses and commentaries it draws on.

For families, students and teachers: anyone who wants to see the parsha, not only read it.
